## slice_layers

Per-layer data of a sliced plate (layers 1-based as the preview slider shows): z, height, time, extruded volume, highest volumetric flow and speed, fan and temperature, tool changes, whether the layer has support / bridges / overhang walls; `columns` adds roles (per feature) and filaments. Paged: `from`, `to`, `every`, `limit` (max 200; `next` when more). With detail "section" and `layer` or `z` (optionally an XY `box` in plate coordinates): the cross-section of one layer from the moves (features, lengths, widths, filled area) and from the sliced geometry (islands, area, wall count and thickness, measured infill density, regions with their infill settings). Fails with not_sliced or busy_slicing.

## app_status

Status of the OrcaSlicer window this server drives: application version, printer (preset, bed size, nozzles, plate type) and process presets in use, project name and unsaved state, filaments, what is busy right now (slicing, running jobs, an open dialog or gizmo, and whether changes are blocked), the next undo and redo step, the folders files may be read from and written to, and the coordinate and id conventions. No arguments.

## history

Undo, redo and the change journal. action "list" (default) returns the names of the undo steps (most recent first), the redo steps, and the journal: changes the undo stack does not cover (preset edits, filament list and colours), newest first. Every change made through this server is one undo step named "MCP: ...", mixed with the user's own steps. action "undo" / "redo" moves `steps` steps (default 1); action "revert" reverts the journal entries in `journal_seq`. Both return a scene summary. Undo also reverts the user's steps if they are on top: check the names first.

| Argument | Type | |
|---|---|---|
| `action` | `list` \| `undo` \| `redo` \| `revert` | default `"list"` |
| `steps` | integer | How many steps to undo or redo.; 1..50, default `1` |
| `journal_seq` | integer[] | For action "revert": seq numbers of journal entries.; at least 1 |
| `if_rev` | integer | Fail with conflict if the scene rev changed. |

## job_status

Progress and result of a long-running operation started by another tool (slice, arrange, orient, mesh operations): status running | finished | failed | cancelled, percent, status text and, when finished, the result. Without `job` it reports the most recent job (optionally of one `kind`). With wait_s > 0 the call waits until the job ends or the time is up, so one call replaces a polling loop.

| Argument | Type | |
|---|---|---|
| `job` | string | a job id such as job3, as returned by the tool that started it |
| `kind` | string | Without `job`: only consider jobs of this kind, e.g. "slice". |
| `wait_s` | number | Wait up to this many seconds for the job to end.; 0..280, default `0` |

## filament_edit

Change the project's filament slots, as the sidebar's filament list does. action add {color?: "#RRGGBB" (default: the next colour the app picks), preset?: filament preset for the new slot}: limit at 64 slots. action remove {filament, replace_with?}: a slot in use by objects, parts, height ranges or colour paint needs replace_with (the slot that takes them over), else in_use; the last filament cannot be removed (last_filament); slots above move down by one (see notes). action set_color {filament, color}: the slot's colour, as the colour picker sets it (objects using it are redrawn). action auto_flush: recompute the flushing volumes matrix. Mixed-colour slots are read-only. The preset of a slot is chosen with preset_select type filament; objects and parts are assigned to a slot with object_update / modifier_update `filament`. Outside the undo stack: recorded in the journal (history revert: add, set_color and auto_flush are revertible, remove is not). Returns every slot with its preset, type, colour and where it is used.

| `action` (required) | `add` \| `remove` \| `set_color` \| `auto_flush` |  |

| `replace_with` | integer | Slot that takes over what used the removed slot (remove).; >= 1 |

## mesh_query

Measure and inspect model geometry without changing it. kind "stats": facets, volume, surface area, whether the mesh is closed (manifold), size and bounding box of objects or parts. "overhangs": the area that faces downward within threshold_deg of straight down (the support gizmo's angle test, but faces lying on the bed are left out; paint_apply's overhang selector includes them), with the largest connected regions and their boxes - use it to judge whether and where supports are needed before slicing. "flat_faces": the faces an object could be laid on (planes of its convex hull, largest first) with their normals, for choosing an orientation. "raycast": what a ray hits - give a `pixel` of a screenshot together with that screenshot's `camera`, or an `origin` and `direction` - returning the object, part, facet, point and surface normal; this turns "the thing I see at pixel (520, 300)" into a point on the model. "distance": between two points. Positions and boxes are in plate coordinates (mm).

| `pixel` | [Vec2](#shared-types) | raycast: [u, v] in a screenshot, from its top-left corner. |

## modifier_add

Add a body to an object, as the object list's context menu does: type part, negative_part, modifier (a region with its own settings), support_blocker, support_enforcer, or a precise-seam body (precise_seam_center|left|right fix the seam inside the body; _enforced|_blocked|_neutral retype seam candidates). Shape: a primitive {primitive: cube|cylinder|sphere|cone|disc|torus|slab, size} with sizes in mm (cube [x,y,z] or one edge; cylinder/cone/disc [diameter,height]; sphere [diameter]; torus [inner diameter, tube diameter]; slab [x,y,z], default 1.5x the object's footprint and half its height) or a mesh file {file, scale}. The body is aligned with the bed (whatever the object's rotation), then rotated by place.rotation (degrees about the bed's X, Y, Z axes, in that order, around the body's centre). Placement: place.center_rel [0..1]^3 across the object's part box in the object frame ([0.5,0.5,1] = top centre; same numbers as scene_get's center_rel), or place.center with place.frame object (default; the object's own frame, as scene_get volume centres) | plate (as scene_get instance positions) | world. One undo step. Returns the object, the new volume (detail full) and `placement` with the body's box in object, plate and world frames to verify where it went. Move/resize/retype later with modifier_update; delete with `delete`; height ranges are layer_range.

## layer_range

Height-range modifier of an object: settings that apply between two heights (mm from the bed), e.g. a finer layer_height or denser infill for z 0-5. action list | add {z_min, z_max, values} | update {z_min, z_max (identify the range), new_z_min?, new_z_max?, values?, reset?} | delete {z_min, z_max}. A range always has a layer_height (default: the object's or the process's) and takes the keys of a part plus layer_height (settings_search scope layer_range). Ranges of one object must not overlap (overlap); layer_height must be within the printer's min/max layer height. Range settings apply to parts, not to modifiers. One undo step. Returns the object's ranges.

With `path` it saves there and the project now uses that file (Save As), unless copy: true, which writes a copy and leaves the project's file and unsaved state alone. An existing file other than the project's own is replaced only with overwrite: true. Returns {path, bytes, project}.

| Argument | Type | |
|---|---|---|
| `path` | string | .3mf inside a write root (app_status.write_roots). |
| `copy` | boolean | default `false` |
| `overwrite` | boolean | default `false` |

## model_import

Import model files into the current project as new objects: STL, OBJ, 3MF (geometry only; use project_open for a whole project), STEP/STP, AMF, DRC, SVG. Each file becomes its own object(s); as: "single_object" assembles everything imported into one multi-part object. The import questions are arguments: `units` (auto: tiny models that look like metres or inches are scaled up as the GUI proposes; mm: never; inch / m: convert everything), `multipart` (a file whose objects sit at several heights: auto/merge = one object with parts, keep_separate = separate objects), `too_large` (scale_down or keep), `step` tessellation (linear_deflection mm, angle_deflection rad, split_compound). Objects are placed on `plate` (default current) as the GUI places them; call object_arrange afterwards to pack them. One undo step. Returns `imported` objects in scene_get shape, `failed` files, and the questions answered in `dialogs`.

| Argument | Type | |
|---|---|---|
| `paths` (required) | string[] | at least 1 |
| `as` | `objects` \| `single_object` | default `"objects"` |
| `units` | `auto` \| `mm` \| `inch` \| `m` | default `"auto"` |
| `multipart` | `auto` \| `merge` \| `keep_separate` | default `"auto"` |
| `too_large` | `scale_down` \| `keep` | default `"scale_down"` |
| `plate` | [PlateRef](#shared-types) |  |
| `step` | object |  |
| `step.linear_deflection` | number | mm; smaller = finer (default 0.003).; <= 1, > 0 |
| `step.angle_deflection` | number | radians; smaller = finer (default 0.5).; <= 1, > 0 |
| `step.split_compound` | boolean | Split STEP compounds into separate objects. |
| `if_rev` | integer | Fail with conflict if the scene rev changed. |

## export_file

The one tool that writes files other than the project: kind "stl" / "obj" = meshes of objects as placed (all instances; `objects` to choose, merge: false for one file per object named <stem>_<object>.<ext>), "3mf" = a copy of the project (as project_save copy), "gcode" = the sliced G-code of one plate (as sliced: post-processing scripts are not run), "gcode_3mf" = sliced plate(s) as .gcode.3mf (`plate` "all" for every sliced plate). G-code kinds need a valid slice (not_sliced, busy_slicing) and also return `header` (the G-code's statistics comments) and `summary`. ## scene_get

Scene tree of the open project: plates with their objects, instances (position, rotation, scale, bounding box, whether outside the plate), parts and modifiers (type, filament, size, per-part setting overrides), height ranges, paint summary, plus the project, printer, process preset and filaments. Each plate has prime_tower (null when the plate has none): position (wipe_tower_x/y), box and size of the footprint including the brim, height, and source "estimate" or "sliced". Positions are in mm in the frame of the object's plate. detail "summary" gives counts only, "normal" the full tree, "full" adds matrices, per-volume boxes and mesh statistics. Restrict with `plate` or `objects`; large scenes are paged (`truncated`, `next` -> pass as `offset`). The ids returned here are what every other tool takes.

## settings_get

Read setting values at a scope: "process", "printer", "project", {"filament": slot}, {"plate": n}, {"object": id}, {"volume": id} or {"layer_range": {object, z_min, z_max}}. With `keys`, returns those values as stored at the scope (null when the scope does not set the key). With effective: true, returns for each key the value in force for that item and where it comes from (process, plate:1, object:o57, volume:v77 ...): narrower scopes override wider ones. Without `keys`, returns what is modified at the scope: for a preset the keys that differ from the saved preset, for an object, part or range its overrides. Values: numbers, booleans, strings; percentages as "15%"; lists for per-filament or per-extruder options.

## settings_set

Set or reset print settings at one scope, several keys at once (validated together; nothing is written when one is wrong). scope: "process" | "printer" | {"filament": slot} edit the selected preset as the settings tabs do (it becomes modified, not saved; dependent options adjust, reported in side_effects; auto_adjust answers the app's "adjust automatically?" questions); "project" (project-level values such as flush_volumes_matrix, and curr_bed_type: the plate type for every plate, as the Prepare tab's plate-type selector sets it; app_status printer.bed_type shows it); {"plate": n} (that plate's own curr_bed_type override, print_sequence, spiral_mode, skirt_start_angle, *_sequence_choice auto); {"object": id} | {"volume": id} (part or modifier) | {"layer_range": {object, z_min, z_max}} override the global value for that item, as the object list's settings do. Narrower scopes win: object < part/modifier, layer range applies to parts. `values` {key: value}: numbers, booleans, strings, enums as their value string, percentages "15%", arrays (or one value for every entry) for per-filament/extruder options. `reset`: keys back to the saved preset value (preset scopes) or remove the override (other scopes). Errors: unknown_key (nearest keys), key_not_in_scope (lists the scopes that take the key), invalid_value (lists accepted values), out_of_range (min/max), forbidden_key (post-processing, print host, plugin, and G-code text unless the user allowed it). settings_search finds keys. Object/part/range/plate changes are one undo step; preset and project changes go to the journal (history revert). Returns changed [{key, old, new}] and side_effects; when the application stores a different value than asked (e.g. wipe_tower_x/y, the prime tower's front-left corner, is kept on the plate with its brim and a margin), the entry also has `requested` and a note explains it.

| Argument | Type | |
|---|---|---|
| `scope` (required) | [Scope](#shared-types) |  |
| `values` | object | key -> value. Numbers, booleans, strings; percentages as "15%"; arrays for per-filament or per-extruder options (a single value is applied to every entry). |
| `reset` | string[] | Keys to reset: at preset scopes back to the saved preset value, at object/part/layer/plate scopes remove the override. |
| `auto_adjust` | boolean | Accept the dependent changes the app proposes (reported in side_effects).; default `true` |
| `if_rev` | integer | Fail with conflict if the scene rev changed. |

## plate_validate

Pre-slice check of a plate without slicing: objects partly outside the plate, empty plate, filament / nozzle / plate-type incompatibilities, object collisions, and the slicer's own configuration validation (Print::validate). Returns {ok, errors, warnings, state}; each error has `message`, `kind`, the `object` ({id, name}) or setting `key` it concerns, and a `fix`. Errors are what makes the window's Slice button unavailable; warnings do not block slicing. The current plate is checked fully; another plate gets the geometric checks and its last applied print (slice selects it and re-checks).

| Argument | Type | |
|---|---|---|
| `plate` | [PlateRef](#shared-types) | Default: the current plate. |

## slice

Slice one plate (default: the current one) or all plates, exactly as the Slice button does (the window switches to the Preview tab). Runs as a job: waits up to `wait_s` seconds and returns the finished job with a per-plate summary (time, filament, layers, warnings), or {status: running, job, percent, text}; then poll slice_status. Streams progress notifications when the client asks. A plate that already has a valid result is not re-sliced (`reused`). Fails before starting with validation_failed (the errors, each with a fix), nothing_to_slice or busy_slicing. A change to the scene or settings while slicing stops the slice (status cancelled, cancel_reason scene_changed_or_user): slice again. Results: slice_result, slice_layers, analyze; pictures: screenshot view=preview; file: export_file kind gcode or gcode_3mf.

| Argument | Type | |
|---|---|---|
| `plate` | [PlateRef](#shared-types) or `"all"` | Plate to slice, or "all". Default: current. |
| `wait_s` | number | Seconds to wait for the result before returning status running.; 0..280, default `100` |

## slice_status

Progress of the running slice and the state of every plate: empty, not_sliced, slicing, sliced, invalidated (a change after the last slice), invalid (validation errors), failed (with the error), cancelled. With `wait_s` it long-polls until the running slice ends or the time is up, streaming progress. Returns the latest slice job ({job, status, percent, text, elapsed_s, result}) plus `plates`.

| Argument | Type | |
|---|---|---|
| `wait_s` | number | Wait up to this long for a running slice to end.; 0..280, default `0` |

## slice_cancel

Stop the running slice (as the Cancel button of the slicing notification does). Fails with not_running when nothing is being sliced. The plate keeps no result; slice again to get one.

No arguments.

## slice_result

Figures of a sliced plate, from the slicer's own statistics: `summary` (print time normal/silent, prepare and first-layer time, filament m / g / cost, layers and layer heights, height, filament and extruder changes, travel, support / prime tower / flush volume, toolpath box in plate coordinates), `filaments` (per slot: model, support, tower, flush volumes, length, weight, cost), `features` (per feature: time and share, path length, volume, filament), `objects` (per object instance: weight, volume, support weight, box), `warnings` (slicer warnings with the object, toolpaths outside, collisions, nozzle reach). Fails with not_sliced (state and reason in details, e.g. invalidated after a change) or busy_slicing. Per-layer data: slice_layers; checks: analyze.

| Argument | Type | |
|---|---|---|
| `plate` | [PlateRef](#shared-types) |  |
| `sections` | `summary` \| `filaments` \| `features` \| `objects` \| `warnings`[] | default `["summary", "warnings"]` |
| `time_mode` | `normal` \| `silent` | Time mode for per-feature times.; default `"normal"` |

## screenshot

Picture of the scene from any viewpoint, rendered offscreen with a camera of its own: the user's view is not touched and the window does not have to be visible. view "editor" shows the objects of one plate as in the Prepare tab, on a plain bed (10 mm grid, bold lines every 50 mm, X axis red, Y green, Z blue from the plate origin). Camera: a preset (iso, iso_left, iso_back, iso_back_left, top, bottom, front, back, left, right) or azimuth_deg / elevation_deg (azimuth 0 looks from the front, 90 from the right; elevation 90 looks straight down), a target ("plate", "objects", ids, or a point in plate coordinates), zoom (1 fits the target) and perspective or orthographic projection. Returns the image and, as data: the camera actually used (pass it to mesh_query raycast to turn a pixel into a point on the model), for each object its pixel position and pixel box, and the legend (which colour means what). Pixels are (u right, v down) from the top-left corner. view "preview": the sliced toolpaths of the plate (slice first), drawn offscreen without touching the Preview tab. preview.color_by picks the colouring (feature, filament, speed, flow, layer_time, fan_speed, temperature, layer_height, line_width ...); layers {from, to} (1-based, as the layer slider) or z_range limit what is drawn; section true draws only the top layer of the range (a cross-section; use camera preset top); show toggles travel, seams, retractions, wipes, tool changes; hide_features hides features by key (support, sparse_infill ...). The result's legend gives each colour's meaning (feature with time share, or the value range). editor.overlay draws extra information over the parts - support_paint: support enforcers (green) and blockers (red) as painted, flat over the parts; seam_paint: seam enforcers (blue) and blockers (red); color_paint: colour painting, flat in the filament colours; fuzzy_paint: fuzzy skin painting (green); overhang: faces within editor.overhang_threshold_deg (default 45) of facing straight down, in orange, including faces on the bed (as the support painting gizmo).
